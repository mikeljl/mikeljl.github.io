Jialin Lu

I am Jialin Lu (Mike), a Computer Science Ph.D. student at Simon Fraser University, advised by Wuyang Chen. Previously, I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of British Columbia. My research focuses on AI for Mathematics and formal reasoning, building LLMs to reason with precision and rigor in formal languages like Lean.
